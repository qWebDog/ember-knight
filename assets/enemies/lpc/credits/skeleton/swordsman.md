# Skeleton swordsman
Original artwork: Universal LPC Character Generator, revision 86a3719ee4a7e2e9fb27c7c152e29a741d3e930a, https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator. Body and head are the same skeleton artwork credited in CREDITS.txt and the source definitions in this directory. The steel arming sword comes from the original LPC weapon layers; see the warrior equipment source credits in this project. The SVGs composite the original layers, with centered 64px body cells in the 128px slash atlas. No source pixels were redrawn. Distributed under the applicable original LPC licenses (see OGA-BY-3.0.txt and CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt in the parent LPC directory).
