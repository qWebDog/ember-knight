# Red Dragon sprite attribution

Source: [Red Dragon](https://opengameart.org/content/red-dragon), uploaded by **ZaPaper** (18 November 2017).

- Original artwork: **Buko Studios**, a commissioned asset for **PlayCraft** (playcraftapp.com).
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Original files: `reddragonfly.png`, `reddragonfly2.png`, `reddragonfly3.png`, `reddragonfly4.png`.
- Files are stored in their original 820×644 pixel resolution, without modification. A 4×4 atlas comprises cells of 205×161 pixels; one known missing frame is deliberately skipped by the animation controller.
- Integration: animated enemy dragon in the optional final boss encounter following the original three waves of Ember Knight.

The four sheets cover left, front, right, and rear flying poses. Other characters and equipment remain unaffected.
