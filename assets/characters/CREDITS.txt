SPRITE GEOMETRY
Each preset PNG is 128x256 pixels, transparent.
Tile size: 64x64. Two idle frames per row.
Rows: 0=back/north, 1=left/west, 2=front/south, 3=right/east.
Default front crop: x=0, y=128, width=64, height=64.
Use nearest-neighbor / image-rendering: pixelated when scaling.
